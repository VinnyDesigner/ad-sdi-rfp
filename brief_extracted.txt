AD-SDI Portal — UI Design Brief for Designer
Department of Government Enablement (DGE) · Spatial Data Strategy Implementation · 16 October 2026
1. The job
Design 15 premium, high-fidelity desktop screens for the AD-SDI Portal — Abu Dhabi’s government geospatial data platform, owned by the Department of Government Enablement (DGE). Each screen is a single, fixed-size frame that will be captured as an image and printed on an A4 page.
1.1 What you deliver
A Figma file (or equivalent) with a shared design system and 15 screens, plus 3 Arabic versions.
A view link where each screen opens at exact frame size, one screen per frame.
PNG exports of every frame at 2x. These are sharper than screenshots and are preferred.
1.2 Frame size — the most important rule
Every screen must fit one fixed frame with no scrolling. Nothing may continue below the frame edge.

Exact A4 landscape ratio (1 : 1.414). Fills an A4 landscape page, or sits cleanly at full width on an A4 portrait page

The screens will be opened from your link and captured. To make that clean:
In the view link, set every frame to open at 100% with no browser chrome, no Figma toolbars, no comments, no cursor.
Name each frame exactly as in the screen list (e.g. S01 Home), so frames are easy to find.
Also send the 2x PNG pack named S01_Home_EN.png, S01_Home_AR.png, etc.

2. Visual direction — what “premium” means here
The target feel is calm, precise, sovereign: a world-class government data platform, not a startup dashboard. Premium comes from restraint, consistency and detail — not from effects.
2.1 Mood in three words
Trusted · Intelligent · Sovereign. Deep navy authority, a single warm gold accent, generous white space, real Abu Dhabi geography.
2.2 Reference quality bar (for feel only — do not copy)
Stripe and Linear dashboards — hierarchy, spacing, tabular numbers, quiet colour.
Apple Maps and Mapbox Studio — clean basemaps, elegant map overlays.
Palantir / Bloomberg-style command views — dense information that still reads at a glance (dark screens only).
Leading government portals (e.g. UK GOV.UK, Singapore OneMap) — clarity and accessibility.
2.3 Premium techniques to use
One accent colour. Gold appears in at most 3–4 places per screen: active nav marker, one key highlight, focus ring, one chart emphasis.
Layered depth, not heavy shadows. Hairline 1px borders plus a very soft shadow; floating map panels get a second, softer elevation.
Tabular numbers. All KPI and table figures use tabular figures, right-aligned, with units in smaller grey text.
Hero imagery with purpose. Aerial or 3D views of Abu Dhabi, darkened with a navy gradient, overlaid by subtle cartographic contour lines at 6–8% opacity.
Micro-details. Status dots with a soft halo ring, sparklines inside KPI cards, small “Updated 09:30 GST” stamps, keyboard hint chips (⌘K), skeleton-free final states.
Consistent rhythm. 8 px spacing grid; identical card padding (24 px) and radii everywhere.
Real content. Real place names, plausible numbers, proper Arabic. No lorem ipsum ever.
Dark mode for the two “wow” screens — Digital Twin (S10) and Command Centre (S11) — to create contrast in the set.
2.4 Avoid
Neon gradients, rainbow charts, heavy drop shadows, 3D clip-art, stock-photo people, emoji.
More than one icon style or more than two typefaces.
Crowding. If a screen feels busy, remove a card — don’t shrink text.
Google or Apple map styling or logos. Any copyrighted characters or brands.

Never redraw, recolour, stretch, rotate, crop or add effects to the DGE logo. Clear space around it = height of the emblem.
Product wordmark: “AD-SDI Portal” in Inter 600, with a thin divider line between it and the DGE logo. Optional small subtitle: “National Geospatial Data Clearinghouse”.
No other company logos inside any screen (no IST, Esri, cloud vendor or partner logos).
Logo sizes: top bar 36–40 px tall; login screen 48–56 px tall.

4. Frame, grid and navigation
Two shells cover all screens: a Public shell (S01, S02, S13) and a Workspace shell (S03–S12, S14). S00 Login has its own layout. Desktop screens only — no mobile screens.
4.1 Grid and spacing
Frame 1440 × 1018. Spacing on an 8 px scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48.
Workspace content area: 1440 − 240 (side nav) = 1200 px wide; 32 px padding left/right → 1136 px usable; 12 columns, 24 px gutters.
Public content area: max 1200 px centred, 12 columns, 24 px gutters.
Vertical budget (workspace): top bar 64 · page header 88 · content 842 · bottom stamp line 24. Plan cards to fit this.
Radii: 8 px inputs, buttons, cards · 12 px floating panels, modals · full pill for chips and badges.
Elevation: Level 1 cards = 1px #E3E8EF border + shadow 0 1 2 rgba(16,24,40,0.06). Level 2 floating = shadow 0 12 32 rgba(16,24,40,0.14).

Only licensed or self-made images. Preferred: aerial/satellite views or 3D renders of Abu Dhabi coastline, skyline at dusk, mangroves, road networks.
Treatment: navy gradient overlay (left 90% → right 10%), plus a cartographic contour-line pattern at 6–8% white.
No stock photos of people.
5.4 Maps
Light screens: very light grey vector basemap (warm grey water #DCE3EA, land #F4F5F7, roads white, labels Text secondary).
Dark screens: dark basemap (land #101C2E, water #0A1424, roads #1E2E47, labels white 50%).
Real geography: Abu Dhabi Island, Saadiyat, Yas, Al Maryah, Khalifa City, Mussafah, Al Ain, Al Dhafra.
Data layers: 30–40% fill + 1.5 px stroke in module or status colour; max 4 layers visible.
Always include: zoom +/−, home, layer button, scale bar, north arrow, coordinate readout bottom-left (“24.4539° N, 54.3773° E”), small attribution “Basemap: AD-SDI”.
Map control buttons: 36 px white squares, 8 px radius, Level 1 shadow, stacked with 1px separators.
5.5 Charts
Gridlines very light (#EEF1F5), no chart borders, axis labels Small secondary.
Line charts 2 px, with soft area fill 8%. Target lines dashed, labelled “Agreed target”.
Use module colours for series; status colours only for status.
Donuts: 16 px ring, centre total in KPI style.
Every chart has a title (H3) and a small “Last 30 days ▾” selector at top-right.

11. Arabic, content rules and delivery
11.1 Arabic (RTL) versions — S00, S01, S13 (+ S07 if done)
Mirror the whole layout: nav, logo position, alignment, icons with direction (arrows, chevrons, back).
Do not mirror: maps, logos, charts’ time axes, media/play controls, numbers, code.
Numbers stay 0–9 (Western Arabic digits).
Use real Arabic text, properly joined, typeset in IBM Plex Sans Arabic. A native Arabic reader must check every string before delivery.
Language toggle shows “ع” as active.
11.2 Content rules
Real Abu Dhabi places and real government entity short names (DMT, EAD, SCAD, DoH, ADEK, ADPHC, ADCD) as data publishers. Never show an entity as failing — use at most one amber “At risk” per screen.
People: initials avatars and generic first names with initial only (“Fatima A.”, “Khalid M.”, “Omar R.”). No photos of people.
Dates: screens show a “today” of 14 September 2027. Keep dates consistent across screens.
Numbers must agree across screens (e.g. 2,480 datasets, 64 entities everywhere).
No prices, AED amounts or fees on any screen.
No lorem ipsum, no “Company Name”, no placeholder grey boxes in final frames.
Every screen ends with the micro-line “Illustrative data — conceptual design” bottom-right.
11.3 Accessibility
Text contrast at least 4.5 : 1 (3 : 1 for 18 px+ text).
Status always shown with icon or word, not colour alone.
At least one visible focus ring (gold) somewhere in the set.
